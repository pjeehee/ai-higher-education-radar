# v6.9 Balanced Discovery Policy
1. Run separate domestic university, international university, regulator/QA, sector-leader, and public-policy streams.
2. Within each stream, search corpus GAPs before dense topics.
3. Prefer a new independent institution/source family over a fifth repetitive item from the same source.
4. Preserve leading institutions as anchors, but never let anchor volume stand in for sector prevalence.
5. Require cross-source corroboration before labeling a trend STRONG.
6. Track Korean implementation evidence separately from overseas transferability.
7. Webinars, promotional events, vendor marketing, and unsourced commentary do not enter the core Evidence corpus.
8. Re-run source-balance and confidence audits after every material corpus expansion.
